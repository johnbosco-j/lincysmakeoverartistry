Meet the artist

Hi, I'm Lincy — I make you feel like the most beautiful version of yourself.

I'm a certified professional celebrity makeup artist, hair stylist and saree drapist based in Ambattur, Chennai. Over the last 3+ years I've had the joy of getting 200+ women ready for their biggest moments — weddings, receptions, concept shoots and the bright lights of television, including a season with Super Singer 11 on Vijay TV.

My philosophy is simple: skin first, then soul. Every look is built around your features, your skin tone and your outfit, using high-quality international products and professional techniques — so it photographs beautifully and still feels light and comfortable all day.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How early should I book for my wedding?

Wedding and muhurtham dates fill up quickly, especially in peak season. Reach out as soon as your date is fixed so it can be reserved for you.

Where are you based, and do you travel?

I'm based in Ambattur, Chennai and I'm travel friendly — home, hotel or venue, anywhere in Chennai, across Tamil Nadu and the rest of South India. Share the location while enquiring and I'll confirm travel details.

Looking for the best bridal makeup artist in Chennai or South India?

Choosing the right artist comes down to three things: training, consistency and how comfortable you feel on the day. I'm internationally certified by Raphael Oliver and trained at B3 Bridal Studio, Sheema's Makeover, AAFT and PLUSH Academy, I've styled 200+ clients including contestants on Super Singer 11 (Vijay TV), and every look starts with a consultation so it suits your features, skin and outfit. Browse the portfolio, read the reviews, and message me — a quick chat tells you more than any label.

What's the difference between Classic, HD and Airbrush?

Classic is a beautiful bridal finish with makeup, hairstyling and saree draping included. HD uses finer, high-definition products that photograph flawlessly and comes with complimentary add-ons like premium lashes, hair accessories, extensions and contact lens. Airbrush gives the lightest, longest-wearing and most flawless finish of all.

Can I get a trial before the big day?

Absolutely. A trial session helps us finalise the look, shades and hairstyle so you feel fully confident on the day — ask for it when you book.

Can you get my family and bridesmaids ready too?

Yes — bridesmaid, family and group bookings are customised so everyone complements the bride. Message for a combined quote.

My skin is sensitive / oily / dry — will the makeup suit me?

Every look starts with skin prep tailored to your skin type, using high-quality international products. Let me know about any sensitivities during the consultation.
